### Part 3: Statutory Anchors under the Nigerian Copyright Act 2022

#### 3.1 Part III: Performers’ Rights (§§63–74 NCA 2022)
The 143 Authentic Audio Tokens embody live, unscripted human cultural performances. The contributors—**Auntie Yẹmí**, master drummer **Baba Sẹ́fíù Adégbìlú**, and the Lead Architect's **Father**—are statutory **Performers** under Section 63 of the Nigerian Copyright Act 2022.

* **Exclusive Commercial Rights (§63(1))**:
  Under Section 63(1)(a)–(f), performers enjoy the exclusive right to control:
  - The recording and fixation of their live performance;
  - The direct or indirect commercial reproduction of their fixations;
  - The commercial distribution, rental, and public broadcast of their fixations; and
  - The making available of their fixations to the public by electronic transmission in a commercial setting.
* **Inalienable Moral Rights (§65)**:
  Under Section 65(1), the performers have the perpetual and inalienable right to:
  - Be identified as the performers of their traditional vocalizations and drum rhythms; and
  - Object to any distortion, mutilation, or unauthorized derivative modification that would prejudice their professional honor or cultural standing—including **unauthorized synthetic AI voice cloning or automated speech synthesis (TTS) harvesting**.
* **Statutory Infringement & Actionable Remedies (§71 & §74)**:
  Any corporate entity, AI lab, or scraper that extracts these audio streams for commercial training or redistributes them outside the web application commits a statutory civil and criminal infringement under Section 71, triggering statutory damages, conversion accountings, and injunctive relief.

#### 3.2 Statutory Exceptions: Fair Dealing & Educational Cultural Sanctuary (§68 NCA 2022)
Section 68(1)(a) of the NCA 2022 provides an express safe harbor:
> *"The rights of a performer under this Part are not infringed by... private use, research, educational activities, reporting of current events, or incidental inclusion..."*

By streaming optimized audio directly in the web client without fee, Àgbà Engine operates as an authorized **Educational & Cultural Sanctuary**. The everyday seeker's personal listening, pronunciation practice, and cultural study constitute non-infringing fair dealing under Section 68 and Section 20 of the Act.

#### 3.3 Part IX: Expressions of Folklore (§75 NCA 2022)
Under Section 75, traditional cultural expressions (proverbs, oral narratives, folk rituals, drum languages) belong to the collective cultural heritage of the community. Àgbà Engine upholds Section 75 by:
1. Maintaining **Source Community Attribution Parity**;
2. Refusing to claim private monopoly ownership over raw proverbs or Odù Ifá verses; and
3. Protecting the compilation, structural taxonomy, and performer recordings from unauthorized commercial expropriation.
