### Type A: Sacred Initiatory Secret (*Awo*) Breach
- **Definition**: An allegation that an entity, transcription, or commentary exposes restricted liturgical passwords, esoteric initiatory oaths, secret society covenants (e.g. *Ògbóni*, *Awo Ifá*, *Ẹlẹ́gbẹ́*), or private ceremonial rites never intended for profane disclosure.
- **Immediate Safeguard**: **Instant Soft Quarantine**. The disputed entity is temporarily hidden from the public client (`/web`) and public API responses within **12 hours** while a full council audit is conducted.

### Type B: Lineage Custody & Attribution Parity Dispute
- **Definition**: A claim by a sovereign lineage, royal dynasty, or master guild that an artifact, textile pattern, architectural element, or historical chronogram has been attributed to the wrong craftsman, royal workshop, or geographic township, or that their sovereign custody has been erased.
- **Immediate Safeguard**: Acknowledgment within 12 hours; provisional banner added noting: *"Attribution details currently undergoing traditional council provenance review."*

### Type C: Third-Party Copyright or Museum License Dispute
- **Definition**: A formal claim by an institution, photographer, or publisher alleging that an indexed visual asset, audio recording, or textual quotation exceeds fair dealing (Section 20, NCA 2022) or violates third-party license boundaries.
- **Immediate Safeguard**: Visual or audio token is unlinked from the public CDN within 24 hours pending license verification against our CC0 / Category A / Category B registers.

### Type D: Historiographical, Theological or Orthographic Contest
- **Definition**: A challenge by academic linguists, historians, or cultural societies asserting that a diacritic transcription, homograph disambiguation, historical timeline, or etymological root reflects a contested viewpoint rather than established consensus.
- **Immediate Safeguard**: The entity's metadata is provisionally flagged with Fort Legis's **"Contested"** or **"Insufficient Evidence"** confidence tier in the retrieval index.

## 📬 5. Notice Submission Requirements & Contact Endpoints

To be valid, a **Notice of Cultural Dispute or Request for Redress (NCDRR)** must contain:
1. **Claimant Identity**: Full name, title/affiliation (e.g., lineage elder, authorized family representative, academic researcher, or legal counsel), and verified contact information.
2. **Specific Disputed Entity**: Exact title, URI, or ID of the record within Àgbà Engine.
3. **Exact Ground for Objection**: Detailed explanation under Type A, B, C, or D.
4. **Primary Evidence**: Academic citation, archival documentation, or traditional lineage authorization demonstrating legitimate interest or sacred custody.
5. **Requested Remedy**: Specific request (quarantine, attribution correction, confidence re-tiering, or de-indexing).
6. **Declaration of Good Faith**: A statement made under penalty of professional dishonor or perjury that the information in the notification is accurate.
